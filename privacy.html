Tāne's Ascent: Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

Tāne's Ascent collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved journey, settings and key bindings are stored only on your own device.
  • If you choose "Export save" or "Download report", the file is created on your device and goes nowhere unless you send it yourself.

What the app does not do

  • It does not need an internet connection, and the Android app asks for no permissions.
  • It does not share or sell any data, because it collects none.
  • It contains no third-party advertising or analytics.

Children

The game is made to teach Māori mythology to rangatahi and anyone who wants to learn. Because nothing is collected, no information about children is collected either.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if ads are added), this page will be updated before the change is released.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/stacywaikato-prog/tanes-ascent.
