Tāne's Ascent

Ngā Kete o te Wānanga

Turn your phone sideways and tap Full screen to play.

  • ←→ move
  • Space jump
  • X strike
  • F heavy
  • R burst
  • C light (hold to charge)
  • Shift roll
  • Q lore
  • Esc menu
  • Keys can be changed in Settings

Tāne climbs the twelve heavens to bring Ngā Kete o te Wānanga to the people, while his brother Whiro sends the creatures of darkness against him. Tellings, names and the number of heavens differ between iwi. This game follows a common outline and claims no single authority. Progress saves in this browser at every carved pou. Difficulty, key bindings, text size and reduced motion are in Settings.
