## Checks for each device

1. Title screen appears within 5 seconds and the fonts show macrons (ā) correctly.
2. Start a new journey on Assist. The opening cutscene plays and can be skipped.
3. In Rangi 1, follow every carved sign: walk, jump, ledge grab, vine, strike, roll, slide, light.
4. Strike the glyph stone. The gate opens.
5. Pause, open Settings, change music volume and text size, then resume.
6. Reach the end of Rangi 1. The summary screen shows time, falls and stars.
7. Reload the page. **Continue** returns you to Rangi 2 with your abilities.
8. Export the save file, clear site data, import the file, and load it.
9. On phones: the thumbstick follows your thumb, buttons respond, nothing is cut off at the screen edges.
10. Leave the tab for a minute and come back. The game is paused and the music resumes cleanly.
