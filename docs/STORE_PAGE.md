## Title

Tāne's Ascent: Ngā Kete o te Wānanga

## Short description (one line)

Climb the twelve heavens as Tāne and bring the baskets of knowledge to the people.

## Description

Light has entered the world, but the people do not yet know how to thrive. Tāne sets out to climb the twelve heavens to Toi-o-ngā-Rangi, where the three kete of knowledge and two sacred stones are kept. His elder brother Whiro wants them for himself, and sends the creatures of darkness to stop him.

**Early demo.** The whole journey is playable, from the opening to the credits. Art, levels and balance are still being worked on, and your feedback shapes what comes next.

- 13 heavens, each with its own sky, weather, music and creatures
- A taiaha combo, heavy and air strikes, light bolts and a Mana Burst
- Five divine abilities: Light Wings, Celestial Dash, Forest Shield, Mana Burst and Star Spear
- Four guardians and a four-phase battle with Whiro
- Glyph gates to open, secrets to find and 13 carvings that fill a lore book
- Assist, Normal and Hard difficulty; key rebinding, larger text and reduced motion
- Keyboard, gamepad and touch controls; plays in the browser on desktop and phone

Tellings, names and the number of heavens differ between iwi. This game follows a common outline and claims no single authority.

## Controls

Keyboard: arrows or WASD move · Space jump · X strike · F heavy strike · C light (hold to charge) · R Mana Burst · Shift roll · Q lore · Esc menu. Sound can be turned off in Settings → Audio. Every key can be changed in Settings.

Gamepad: A jump · X strike · RT heavy strike · Y light · LT Mana Burst · B roll · Start menu.

Touch: drag on the left to move; JUMP, STRIKE, HEAVY, LIGHT, BURST and ROLL on the right. Turn the phone sideways.
